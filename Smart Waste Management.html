Anomaly detection — signal types

Z-score anomaly (|z| > 2.5σ)
Readings that deviate more than 2.5 standard deviations from the bin's historical mean. Indicates unusual fill spikes relative to expected behaviour.
Rate-of-change anomaly (> 25%/6h)
Fill level changes exceeding 25 percentage points in a single 6-hour window. Flags illegal dumping, equipment failure, or sensor transmission errors.
